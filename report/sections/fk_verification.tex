\subsection{Verification}
\label{sec:fk-verification}

The DH and PoE models were compared at 514 configurations: home, each joint
alone at $\pm\pi/2$, one mixed pose, and 500 uniformly sampled configurations
in $[-\pi,\pi]^6$ (random seed 42). At each configuration,
we computed the full pose Frobenius residual
$\lVert\Delta T\rVert_F=\sqrt{\lVert\Delta p\rVert_2^2+\lVert\Delta R\rVert_F^2}$,
where $\Delta$ denotes the difference between the models,
$\lVert\Delta p\rVert_2$ is the Euclidean position residual (positions in metres),
and $\lVert\Delta R\rVert_F$ is the Frobenius rotation-matrix residual.
The maximum full pose residual was below $10^{-14}$, consistent with
floating-point roundoff.
