\subsection{Verification}
\label{sec:fk-verification}

Using the data above, the PoE, DH and direct URDF models were compared at
514 configurations: home, each joint alone at $\pm\pi/2$, one mixed pose,
and 500 random configurations in $[-\pi,\pi]^6$.
Across all model pairs, the largest position residual was
$5.11\times10^{-11}$ m and the largest rotation-matrix Frobenius residual
was $1.18\times10^{-11}$. The DH--PoE residuals were below $10^{-14}$;
the URDF stores the fixed tilt as a decimal approximation of $\pi/6$.
