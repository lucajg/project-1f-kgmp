\subsection{Verification}
\label{sec:fk-verification}

Using the data above, the PoE, DH and direct URDF models were compared at
514 configurations: home, each joint alone at $\pm\pi/2$, one mixed pose,
and 500 random configurations in $[-\pi,\pi]^6$.
Across all model pairs, the pose-matrix Frobenius residuals were below
$10^{-14}$. The MATLAB URDF transcription uses the exact tilt $\pi/6$;
a separate read-only check compares its joint placements, axes and chain
with the supplied URDF, allowing $10^{-10}$ for decimal truncation.
